SHOPEASE — RETURN POLICY

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Return eligibility
Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. The product must be unused and returned in its original packaging.

2. Damaged products
Customers who receive a damaged product must contact customer support within 7 calendar days of delivery. They should provide their order number and a description of the damage.

3. Non-returnable products
Personalized products and downloadable digital products are not eligible for return unless required by applicable law.

4. Return approval
Submitting a return request does not automatically approve the return. ShopEase support must review the request and confirm eligibility.

5. Refund timing
After an approved return is received and inspected, the refund is initiated within 5–7 business days. The time taken for the funds to appear in the customer's account may vary by payment provider.

6. Exceptions
If a situation is not covered by this document, customers should contact ShopEase support for clarification. This document does not establish any additional warranty or compensation policy.
