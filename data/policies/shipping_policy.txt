SHOPEASE — SHIPPING POLICY

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Standard shipping
Standard shipping typically takes 3–5 business days after an order has been dispatched. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date.

2. Order tracking
Customers can request an order status or tracking update from customer support by providing their order number.

3. Delays
Delivery may be delayed because of weather, carrier disruptions, public holidays, or operational issues. Customers should use the latest tracking information for updates.

4. Missing tracking information
If tracking information is unavailable, customer support should check the order system before providing a status update.

5. Delivery guarantees
This document does not promise guaranteed delivery dates or automatic compensation for shipping delays. Questions not covered here should be referred to ShopEase support.
